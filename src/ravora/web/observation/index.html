How each number is measured
First commenters
Accounts with a first recorded chat message, a completed reply to them, and another message after that reply in their first recorded broadcast. Silent viewers have no chat identity. Accounts are counted separately by platform.
Viewers and peak
Unique viewers sum per-broadcast platform summaries imported after each broadcast. Peak uses the reported peak when available, then the maximum of live Twitch samples taken about once a minute.
Chat coverage
Time with a connected chat reader divided by the broadcast window. Slot comparison rates use only finished broadcasts with at least 114 minutes of chat observation and 95% coverage.
Market and overlap
Every five minutes during scheduled windows, Twitch streams in Just Chatting are filtered by the slot language and VTuber tags. Market averages exclude Ray and use completed scans. Top five share is the five largest audiences per scan divided by all viewers in those scans. Tags are self-reported. Overlap pairs consecutive samples of Ray and vedal987 across all categories.
Broadcast state
Live requires recent provider evidence and expires on this page after 150 seconds. A provider summary or confirmed end closes a broadcast. Unconfirmed records stay out of finished totals.
Ray gap
Silence between the last audible sample of one Ray utterance and the first of the next, when the comment was already waiting. Below 20 intervals the p95 is the sample value and is marked with its count.
Response times
Comment to on air runs from studio receipt to the first reply audio in the broadcast. Comment to viewer runs from the platform's comment time to audio detected on an independent player.
API use and cost
Calls include retries and discarded prefetched calls. Cost uses estimated USD list prices. Calls without a known price are counted separately.

The studio exports aggregate counts and timings. Account IDs and messages stay with the studio. The page checks for updates every 30 seconds. A failed collection keeps the last good snapshot and its timestamp.
